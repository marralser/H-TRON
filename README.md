# H-TRON for DOS and GEM
Simple H-TRON game with expanded graphic card (DOS), GEM, and Amstrad joystick support

In 2016 RobertK wrote H-TRON, a simple Tron game (https://www.mobygames.com/game/89372/h-tron/). He wrote it on a Schneider Euro PC using Borland TurboC and natively for the Hercules graphics card. Isn't that cool - a native Hercules game developed on the EuroPC in 2016?

The game is simple but fun. However, many computer enthusiasts will be unable to play it on their native hardware, as the HGC has a resolution too high for CGA, EGA and VGA graphic cards. 
So here comes my contribution, a decade later, in 2026:  A H-TRON port for the CGA, EGA and VGA graphic cards. Also written using Borland TurboC, and compiled on an Amstrad/Schneider PC1640 with a 8MhZ NEC V30.

I also added a version GEM, (Graphics Environment Manager), originally from Digital Research. GEM was amazing for its time, but never a huge success. I think this was in part due to a lack of software. Maybe HTRON for GEM would have changed that ;-)

I added one new feature to the DOS version: a  support for the Amstrad PC 1512/1640 digital joystick. Isn't that cool too?  And, while the EGA and VGA versions play extremely similar to RobertKs Hercules original, the CGA version is a bit different, and I think, more fun to play ;-) Try it out.

Please note that I have also worked on a more expanded version of the game, with levels, simple sounds, and some other features. DOS only. Have a look at https://github.com/marralser/H-TRON2

MR, in Sept. 2026

Writing this on the Amstrad PC1640, using MS Word 4.0 for DOS.

- HTRON.exe for the Hercules Graphic Card
- CGAtron.exe for CGA (try that one first :-))
- EGAtron.exe for EGA
- VGAtron.exe for VGA
- HTRONGEM.APP for GEM version 2.0 and newer
